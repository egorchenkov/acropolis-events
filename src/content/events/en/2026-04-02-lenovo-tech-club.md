The Acropolis Integro team — Pavel Tenitskiy, Viacheslav Borisov, and Yury Chebotarev — attended Lenovo Tech Club Tashkent 2026, an annual technology event organized by the Lenovo team at Quadro Restaurant.

The event was organized by the Lenovo team: Alnur Yermagambetov, Dauren Zhunusbekov, Olga Gubina, Maya Poluyan, Grigory Pryalukhin, Alexander Totikov, Denis Kochenkov, and Nino Tsotsoria.

The centerpiece of the program was a technical session by Grigory Pryalukhin dedicated to updates in the Lenovo Infrastructure Solutions Group product lineup. Key changes were presented: a dedicated server class for AI workloads, expansion of the storage portfolio to four classes (DM/DG/DE/DS Series), the updated ThinkSystem V4 server portfolio and ThinkAgile HCI hyperconverged platforms. Lenovo's continued technology partnerships with NVIDIA, Nutanix, Red Hat, SUSE, and Veeam Software were also highlighted.

Special guest Yevgeniy Zossimov from Veeam Software presented the company's updated positioning and product innovations, including the Immutability Storage Appliance — a hardware solution for immutable backup storage.
