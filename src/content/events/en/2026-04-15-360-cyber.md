The Acropolis Integro team participated in 360 Cyber — a full lifecycle cybersecurity conference organized by B&B Security at the JW Marriott Absheron hotel in Baku. The event brought together over 300 guests, 20 speakers, and 30 experts from telecom, finance, oil & gas, and other critical sectors.

Together with partner Commvault, the team presented solutions in cyber resilience and data protection. Evgeny Pukhov covered Cleanroom Recovery, Threat Scan, and the "Real Clean" concept — restoring without losing post-attack improvements. Splunk showcased a unified TDIR platform with Risk-Based Alerting and an AI Assistant; CyberArk outlined the four pillars of Identity Security with Zero Standing Privileges; Opinnate presented its NSPM platform with the Opi AI copilot and a 90% reduction in manual policy audits; and B&B Security covered SOC transformation with agentic AI amid an 89% rise in AI-enabled attacks.

The key takeaway: modern cybersecurity is not a collection of disjointed products but a continuous lifecycle — "Assess → Protect → Detect → Respond → Improve" — that unifies data, identity, and infrastructure protection into a single architecture. The event concluded with two panel discussions and a gala dinner.
