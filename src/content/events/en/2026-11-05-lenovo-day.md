On November 5, 2026, Acropolis Integro participated in **Lenovo Day Tashkent 2026**, Lenovo's conference under the **The Speed to Lead** motto, held at Hilton Tashkent City.

The programme focused on Uzbekistan's journey to an AI-Native Enterprise, global AI-adoption trends, and hybrid-AI infrastructure. The demo zone presented Lenovo's and its partners' solutions, while the agenda included customer stories from AzinTelecom and Ucell together with Veeam.

### Programme and topics

Participants explored the transition from AI experiments to an AI-Native Enterprise, economic impact, infrastructure foundations, data sovereignty, scalability and energy efficiency. Lenovo EMEA presented its approach to data management for hybrid AI, and Intel covered the Hybrid AI journey.

Dedicated sessions covered the full data lifecycle with Scality, the PC market, customer cases, and AI expert Vadim Kirilin's presentation, “AI and the Bitter Lesson: What Comes Next.” The evening programme featured Lenovo&Ducati, a session on technology in football with FIFA guests, networking and a gala dinner.

### Format and languages

The event ran from 10:00 to 22:00 Tashkent time (GMT+5). Working languages were Uzbek, Russian and English, with simultaneous interpretation available throughout the conference.
