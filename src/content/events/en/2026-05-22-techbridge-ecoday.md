> ### Reaching new peaks — sustainably.
>
> **Nature. Technology. Future.** Think globally — act locally.

ACROPOLIS INTEGRO joined the partner event of the year, hosted by [**TechBridge Distribution**](https://techbridge.uz/) in the picturesque Chimgan mountains at **Za'faron Premium Hotel**.

---

### Who was on stage

Leading infrastructure vendors and integrator partners from across the region met in one place:

- [**Lenovo**](https://www.lenovo.com/) — ISG servers, storage, and AI workstations
- [**HPE Networking**](https://www.hpe.com/us/en/networking.html) — unified Juniper + Aruba networking portfolio
- [**H3C**](https://www.h3c.com/) — alternative networking portfolio for DC and campus
- [**Delta**](https://www.deltapowersolutions.com/) — UPS and data center energy efficiency

### The eco concept, in detail

Sustainability ran as a red thread through the entire event:

- 🌿 **Venue design** — wooden vendor logos on a living green wall
- ♻️ **TechBridge ECO Challenge** — collection of new unused goods for charity organizations and used batteries for recycling
- 🏊 **Pool activity** at the resort as part of the eco program

### Technology agenda

Four keynote talks shaped the day — details and speakers are in the **Presentations** section below.

### Value for our partnership

For ACROPOLIS INTEGRO, the event was an opportunity to **deepen the relationship** with TechBridge and vendors across several directions:

| Direction | What we picked up |
|---|---|
| Lenovo ISG | Current roadmap, Top Choice Express, new ThinkStation PGX |
| HPE Networking | Unified Juniper + Aruba portfolio after the M&A, Apstra, Mist AI |
| H3C | «AI in All»: AI-ECO/AI-ECN/AI-SOC, LinSeer, S12500 AI |
| Delta | DPH Gen.3 — 1250 kW in 1 m², 97.5% efficiency, lithium-ion |

The eco format underlined an important point: **sustainable technology** is not only about architecture and performance — it is also about a responsible approach to resources, data center energy efficiency, and a long equipment lifecycle.
