On April 16–17, 2026, the ACROPOLIS INTEGRO team delivered a closed two-day technical training on the **Nutanix** platform for the engineering teams of our customers.

The training was structured not as a product pitch but as a working session — with real-world cases, architectural decisions, and operational nuances that are not found in the official documentation.

### What we covered over two days

**Day 1 — fundamentals and platform internals:**

- HCI theory and differences from classic storage arrays
- AOS internals: CVM, Stargate, Cassandra, metadata
- Data locality as a source of performance
- Prism Element: storage containers, volume groups, hardware layout
- Recommended settings: Rebuild Capacity Reservation, Rack Awareness, Pulse, VIP/DSIP
- RF2 vs RF3, compression, deduplication, erasure coding — when to enable what

**Day 2 — operations and automation:**

- Prism Central: categories, tags, role model, Authorization Policies
- VM deployment from Cloud Image via cloud-init and sysprep
- Flow Network Security and IP-less microsegmentation
- Protection Policy, Consistency Groups, Near-Sync replication
- Recovery Plan with test network and automatic re-IP
- X-Play — low-code workflow automation
- NDB (Nutanix Database Services) — managing Oracle, PostgreSQL, MS SQL, MongoDB
- External Storage (Pure, NetApp, Dell) — when it applies in the region
- Troubleshooting and working with Nutanix Support

### Format

A two-day intensive with a step-by-step flow: theory → architecture → hands-on in the UI → operational recommendations. The 45-minute-block format with breaks proved effective — the material is absorbed without overload, leaving room for questions and post-mortems.

### Who benefits from this training

- IT teams migrating from VMware to Nutanix or evaluating the platform
- Infrastructure architects designing HCI solutions
- DBAs considering NDB for database consolidation
- Teams implementing disaster recovery and microsegmentation

### Outcome for participants

Each participant received a structured memo with core concepts, operational recommendations, and a list of next steps — including the Nutanix NCA certification path and links to Nutanix Bible and Portal.

### Next steps

A lab demonstration with hands-on Protection Policy and Recovery Plan setup. Further sessions are planned on NDB, Flow Network Security, and X-Play automation.
