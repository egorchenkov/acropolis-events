Acropolis Integro participated in the Cyber Security Summit Central Eurasia 2025 — the annual gathering of cybersecurity industry leaders in Central Asia. The event was organized with the support of the National Cybersecurity Center of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

Together with partner Commvault, the Acropolis Integro team presented solutions in the field of cyber resilience and data protection for enterprises across the region.
