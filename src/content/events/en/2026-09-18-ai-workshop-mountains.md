On September 18, 2026, the ACROPOLIS INTEGRO team will host an **off-site hands-on AI workshop** — a day of intensive work with AI technologies away from the office bustle, in the mountains on the shore of the Charvak Reservoir.

The venue is **Avenue Park Hotel** (Yusufkhona): a countryside resort complex overlooking Charvak, 15 minutes from the Chimgan cable car. The change of scenery is part of the format: complex topics sink in better where nothing distracts you.

### What we will cover

- The modern AI landscape and which technologies already deliver value in the enterprise
- **LLM and RAG in practice** — building knowledge bases: "ask the documentation" instead of digging through folders
- **AI agents** — from chatbots to autonomous systems that actually resolve the request
- AI infrastructure: GPU servers, on-premises model hosting, and cost-of-ownership analysis

### Format

A working lab, not a series of presentations: real-world scenarios, live demos, and discussion of participants' own tasks. Seats are limited — this keeps the pace individual and leaves room for questions along the way.

### Who should attend

- IT and digital transformation leaders assessing where AI applies
- Architects and engineers designing AI infrastructure
- Teams ready to move from AI experiments to production deployments
