On June 16–17, 2026, the ACROPOLIS INTEGRO team delivered a two-day hands-on training on the **Commvault** platform for our customers' technical specialists at the company's office in Tashkent.

The training was built around the **Commvault Unity** concept — a single data platform that covers every layer of protection: operational recovery, autonomous recovery, and cyber resilience. The program spanned both product positioning and the engineering side: installation, configuration, policies, agents, and monitoring.

### What we covered over two days

#### Day 1 — product track: from backup to cyber resilience

- The Commvault journey: from backup to cyber resilience
- Operational Recovery — Bronze package
- Autonomous Recovery — Silver package
- Cyber Recovery — Gold package
- Commvault Readiverse — concept and use cases

**Day audience:** IT leaders, infrastructure architects, and those responsible for business continuity and information security.

#### Day 2 — engineering track: architecture, installation, policies

- Commvault architecture
- Installation and setup of core components
- Storage configuration
- Plan: concept and working with policies
- Agent installation and SubClient setup
- Virtualization Backup configuration
- FileSystem Backup configuration
- Database Backup configuration
- Monitoring and reporting

**Day audience:** systems engineers, infrastructure and backup administrators, and specialists in virtualization, databases, and file systems.

### What participants gained

- A holistic understanding of the Commvault stack: from operational backup to cyber resilience and Readiverse
- A map of how the Bronze / Silver / Gold packages align with real protection scenarios
- Hands-on practice installing and configuring key platform components
- Ready-to-use approaches to configuring policies (Plan), agents, and SubClients for real workloads
- Backup scenarios for virtualization, file systems, and databases
- Principles for building monitoring and reporting in operations

### Format

A two-day on-site technical training, 10:00–16:00. A chance to see the platform as a whole — product logic and engineering detail — in one place over two working days.
